WHAT · WHAT IS IT

A back-to-school event that gathers new students from three universities in one place

The UQ QUT Griffith Freshman Welcome Event is designed for new students from UQ, QUT, and Griffith, bringing students, city-life resources, and local brands together during the back-to-school season. This event is jointly hosted by JR Academy and Market Square. Students arrive in concentrated waves to browse booths, collect gifts, complete stamp missions, join prize draws, and ask questions face to face, so exposure, interaction, and conversion all happen in one event.

MECHANIC · DRAW TICKETS

Students earn tickets in four ways

Every interaction leads back to one reward unit: the draw ticket. The more tickets students have, the better their chance to win, which naturally motivates them to browse booths, scan codes, form teams, and share online.

1

Check-in ticket

Each student receives 1 ticket upon arrival and enters the prize pool immediately.

2

Team bonus

Teams of 3 receive 2 tickets each, and teams of 5 receive 3 tickets each, encouraging group sign-ups.

3

Stamp mission

Each merchant booth scan gives 1 ticket, with up to 8 by default, and your store can be one of those check-in points.

4

Social tasks

Additional tickets are granted after verification of Xiaohongshu, WeChat Moments, or Instagram posts.
