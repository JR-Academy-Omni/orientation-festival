JR08 · CHILD-SAFE BY DESIGN
SAFE
RESPONSIBLE YOUTH ENGAGEMENT

Protect young people.
Protect sponsor trust.

Clear consent, privacy and on-site rules protect participants and the brands that support them.

01Consent-based contact

Follow-up uses actively selected channels only.

02Role-based screening

Checks and records match actual responsibilities.

03Separate media consent

Participants can choose not to be photographed.

04Managed interaction

Engagement is open, age-appropriate and supervised.

05Brand content review

Categories, messaging and gifts are approved first.

06School rights respected

Names and logos require written permission.
