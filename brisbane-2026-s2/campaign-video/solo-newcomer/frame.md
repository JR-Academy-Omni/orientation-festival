# Visual direction — Warm Brisbane documentary

## Concept

从一个人待在室内的安静，走向布里斯班下午阳光和真实人群。画面像一段被朋友拍到的新生日记，不像商业广告棚拍。

## Palette

- Ink: `#071921`
- Paper: `#F5FFF9`
- Cyan: `#43DDE6`
- Lime: `#D6FF67`
- Coral: `#FF7866`
- Warm light: `#FFD6A3`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

## Typography

- Chinese display and body: embedded `Hiragino Sans GB`, weights 700 and 900.
- English/date labels: `Inter`, weights 700 and 900.
- Headlines are compact, left-aligned, with 1.05 line-height and no more than two lines.

## Motion language

- Rhythm: quiet hold → decisive push → warm release → clear hold.
- Photography moves with slow 4–7% dolly zooms and small directional pans.
- Copy resolves through soft blur and vertical rise. No bouncing text, fake phone UI, confetti, or particle clutter.
- Scene changes use brief warm overexposure flashes, not repeated logo wipes.

## Composition

- Keep faces unobstructed. Copy occupies image negative space or a controlled dark gradient.
- Use full-bleed photography with foreground gradient depth; avoid floating cards.
- Official logo appears once, in the final scene only.

## Audio

- No voiceover.
- A short whoosh punctuates the decision to leave; a gentle chime supports the final event card.

## Avoid

- No QR codes, URLs, sponsor logos, prize lists, fabricated testimonials, or guaranteed social outcomes.
- No white poster rectangle around the official logo.
- No per-scene title card repetition.
